AM Eats Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

1. Introduction

AM Eats respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use the AM Eats application and services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you provide when creating an account or placing an order, such as your name, email address, phone number, delivery information, and order details.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and improve AM Eats services, process and manage orders, provide customer support, communicate important service information, and help keep the platform secure.

4. Restaurants and Service Providers

Information necessary to complete an order may be shared with participating restaurants and service providers involved in fulfilling the order.

5. Payments

Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers. AM Eats does not intentionally store complete payment card information on its own systems.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

7. Your Choices

You may contact us if you have questions about your information or wish to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or privacy practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page.
